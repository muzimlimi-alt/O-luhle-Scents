Our Story

Crafting Moments Through Scent

TOCA brings you an exclusive collection from Officina Del Toca — a house dedicated to the ancient art of perfumery. Each fragrance is an extrait de parfum, the purest and most concentrated form of perfume.

Our collection spans from the bold and captivating Premium range to the vibrant Essentials, ensuring there is a scent for every personality and occasion. Every bottle is a work of art, designed to evoke emotion and leave a lasting impression.

Based in South Africa, we are committed to making luxury accessible without compromise. From our meticulous sourcing to our elegant packaging, every detail reflects our passion for excellence.
