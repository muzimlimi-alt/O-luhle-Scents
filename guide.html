Chapter One

The Fragrance Pyramid

Every perfume unfolds over time in three distinct acts. Understanding this structure transforms the way you experience — and choose — a fragrance.

Top Notes 0 — 15 min
Heart Notes 15 min — 4 hrs
Base Notes 4 hrs — 24+ hrs

Time on skin →

I
Top Notes — The First Impression

The opening act. These light molecules evaporate quickly — usually within 15 minutes — but they create the all-important first impression. Top notes are typically fresh, bright, and invigorating. Never buy a fragrance based on the top notes alone.

Common: Bergamot · Citrus · Pepper · Green Tea · Aldehydes
II
Heart Notes — The True Character

The soul of the fragrance. Heart notes emerge as the top notes fade and form the dominant scent for several hours. They define what a perfume is fundamentally about — and they're the reason you fall in love with a fragrance.

Common: Rose · Jasmine · Iris · Vetiver · Cardamom · Oud
III
Base Notes — The Lasting Memory

The foundation. Rich, deep, and tenacious — base notes linger on your skin for hours after the heart has faded. They add depth to the overall composition and are what people notice on your clothes the next morning.

Common: Sandalwood · Musk · Amber · Vanilla · Patchouli · Benzoin

Chapter Three

The Scent Families

All fragrances belong to one of seven olfactory families. Knowing your family is the fastest way to find your signature scent — and to discover new ones you'll love.

🌸
Floral

The most popular fragrance family. Floral scents capture the essence of blooming flowers — from single-note soliflores to lush, multi-flower bouquets. They range from powdery and romantic to green and dewy.

Key Notes: Rose, Jasmine, Peony, Tuberose, Lily of the Valley
Date night Spring / Summer Daytime
🍋
Fresh & Citrus

Bright, energising, and clean. Citrus and fresh green notes provide an immediate burst of vitality. These are universally appealing, office-friendly, and perfect for warmer climates.

Key Notes: Bergamot, Lemon, Grapefruit, Neroli, Green Tea, Sea Salt
Work Sport Hot weather
🍑
Fruity

Playful, modern, and vibrant. Fruity fragrances incorporate the essence of ripe and exotic fruits beyond citrus — they're youthful, approachable, and often addictively sweet.

Key Notes: Peach, Pear, Mango, Blackcurrant, Raspberry, Camellia
Casual Everyday Young & joyful
🪵
Woody

Sophisticated, grounding, and timeless. Woody fragrances are built on the warm, dry, sometimes earthy accord of precious woods. They read as confident and refined — equally suited to men and women.

Key Notes: Sandalwood, Cedarwood, Vetiver, Oud, Patchouli, Guaiac
Evening Autumn / Winter Formal
🕌
Oriental & Amber

Opulent, sensual, and unforgettable. Oriental fragrances are rich in resins, spices, and precious woods. They project powerfully and linger long into the night. The definition of a statement fragrance.

Key Notes: Amber, Vanilla, Myrrh, Incense, Cinnamon, Benzoin, Musk
Evening events Winter Statement wear
🌊
Aquatic & Marine

Clean, airy, and refreshing. Aquatic scents evoke the ocean breeze and open skies. They're unisex, modern, and effortlessly cool — ideal for those who prefer an understated, invisible luxury.

Key Notes: Sea Salt, Ozonic Notes, Driftwood, Calone, Cypress, Ambergris
Summer Outdoors Unisex
🌿
Aromatic & Herbal

Natural, calming, and grounded. Aromatic fragrances draw from lavender fields, herb gardens, and green meadows. They possess a meditative quality that feels both masculine and modern.

Key Notes: Lavender, Rosemary, Sage, Thyme, Fougère, Basil, Chamomile
Day wear Meditation Wellness
Chapter Four

How to Choose

The right fragrance depends on where you're going, who you're meeting, and how you want to feel. Use these archetypes as a starting point.

01
☀️
The Daily Signature

Your everyday fragrance should be versatile, clean, and never overwhelming. It should work in closed offices, on public transport, and across seasons. Think subtle sophistication — people smell you and wonder, they don't know exactly what it is.

→ Fresh Citrus, Light Floral, Soft Woody
02
🌙
The Evening Statement

Evening allows you to be bolder. Your skin is warmer, the air cooler, and the setting more intimate. Lean into depth and sensuality — choose something that announces your arrival and stays with those you've spent time with.

→ Oriental, Deep Woody, Rich Floral, Amber
03
💼
The Professional

In professional settings, fragrance should enhance your presence without dominating the room. Choose lower concentration, light formulas, or apply sparingly from a more intense fragrance. Others should need to lean in to notice it.

→ Aromatic, Aquatic, Clean Woody, EDT concentration
04
✨
The Special Occasion

Weddings, milestone dinners, milestone moments. Reach for your finest — your Extrait de Parfum. Fragrances worn at significant events become olfactory memories. Choose something worthy of the moment.

→ Extrait de Parfum, Signature Floral, Oud, Rare Amber
Chapter Five

Fragrance by Season

Temperature affects how fragrance molecules diffuse from your skin. Warm weather amplifies intensity — what smells beautiful in winter can become overwhelming in summer.

Spring

Light and optimistic. As temperatures rise, choose fresh florals and green accords that echo nature's renewal. Avoid very heavy orientals — they can clash with spring's brightness.

Ideal: Floral · Fruity · Green

Summer

Heat amplifies everything. In summer, a light EDT becomes a medium EDP, and an EDP can become overbearing. Choose aquatics, citrus, and fresh accords. Apply to clothing as well as skin for extra longevity in the heat.

Ideal: Aquatic · Citrus · Light Fruity

Autumn

The golden season for fragrance. Cooler air allows deeper, warmer scents to bloom without overwhelming. This is when spicy, woody, and leather notes come alive. The transitional season for your transitional fragrance.

Ideal: Woody · Spicy · Leather

Winter

Cold air suppresses diffusion — this is when bold, rich fragrances earn their place. Extrait de Parfum was made for winter: deep orientals, smoky accords, and rich amber cocooning you in warmth. Layer with a matching body cream for maximum effect.

Ideal: Oriental · Amber · Rich Floral

Chapter Six

The Art of Application

Even the finest Extrait de Parfum will underperform if applied incorrectly. Master these principles and your fragrance will last significantly longer.

Pulse Points

Apply fragrance where body heat will diffuse it continuously throughout the day.

Neck & Décolletage
Best projection — your primary scent cloud
Inner Wrists
Classic application — do not rub together
Behind the Ears
Intimate and lasting — for close encounters
Inner Elbows
Warm crease — releases scent with movement
Behind the Knees
Trail effect as you walk — understated elegance
1
Apply to Moisturised Skin

Fragrance adheres to and lasts significantly longer on hydrated skin. Apply an unscented body lotion immediately after showering, then apply your fragrance while skin is still slightly warm. This creates a perfect base that can extend wear by several hours.

2
Never Rub — Always Let It Settle

Rubbing your wrists together is one of the most common fragrance mistakes. It generates heat and friction that literally breaks down the delicate top note molecules, distorting the fragrance's opening and causing it to fade faster. Spray and allow it to dry naturally.

3
Less is More with Extrait

With Extrait de Parfum concentrations, one to two sprays is almost always sufficient. The beauty of high-concentration fragrance is its ability to fill a room subtly, not announce your arrival from across it. Start with less — you can always add more.

4
Store Your Fragrance Correctly

Heat, light, and humidity degrade fragrance molecules over time. Store your bottles in a cool, dark place — away from your bathroom where steam is common. The original box provides excellent protection. A well-stored fragrance can remain beautiful for years.

5
Give It Time to Develop

Never judge a fragrance in the first five minutes — those are just the top notes, and they're the least representative part of the experience. Allow a full 30 minutes on your skin before deciding. The heart notes that follow are where the true character lives.
